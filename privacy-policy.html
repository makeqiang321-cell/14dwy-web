1. Introduction

Welcome to 14 Days With You Games ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure experience on our website. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our browser-based gaming platform.

2. Information We Collect

14 Days With You Games is designed to respect your privacy. You can access and play all games on this site without creating an account or providing personal details such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Technical Data: When you visit our site, certain technical information may be automatically received, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and basic usage statistics. This data is used solely for performance monitoring and maintaining the security of our platform.

Local Storage: We use your browser's local storage to save your game progress and preferences. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

We may use third-party analytics services to understand how visitors interact with our website. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to gather and analyze information about site usage and to report on activity and trends.

6. Data Security

We take commercially reasonable measures to safeguard the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through our official community channels or contact form on this website.
